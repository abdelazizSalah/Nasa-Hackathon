# Space Mission Design Game (NASA Hackathon)
### Details
- Interactive game
- User will be able to create a Complete Space Mission Design
- Individually or up to 4 participants
    - Individually is easier
    - Multiplayer: HOWWWWWWWW??????????????
- Feedback on mission performance
    - reward effective engineering decisions,
    - efficient resource management,
    - successful collaboration,
    - and achievement of mission objectives.
- We have to develop:
    - creative game mechanics,
    - visualizations,
    - scoring systems,
    - and mission scenarios that make:
        - the experience engaging while requiring players to solve problems,
        - make trade-offs,
        - and collaborate to achieve mission success.
- Create Levels for different knowledge levels
    - Beginner
    - hobbyist
    - Advanced
### Possible things to do in the game
- allow participants to define a mission objective,
- establish mission requirements,
- design a spacecraft,
- select scientific instruments,
- choose a launch vehicle and orbit,
- and manage key constraints such as budget, mass, power, communications, data, and mission lifetime.
Players benefit from seeing how their decisions affect other parts of the
mission, and from understanding the trade-offs involved in real-world space
mission design.

- Responsibilities of the Players
    - mission planning,
    - spacecraft design,
    - science,
    - communications,
    - or resource management

### Game
- Objectives
    - Could be realistic space mission scenarios
    - Should have success and failed Based on SOMETHING
        - Quick Missions
        - Cheap missions
        - At-all-costs missions
- Players can do multiple runs of their design
- They can also tweak it a bit then rerun


### Sequence
1. Select a mission/objective
2. Spacecraft design and the selection of the payload and consequently its size
3. Distance of the mission (Orbits)
    - Explain what choosing an orbit entails
        - coverage,
        - observation opportunities,
        - communications,
        - mission lifetime,
        - and other mission requirements
    - ENSURE COMPATIBLE WITH THE VEHICLE
4. Launch vehicle
    - different payload capacities,
    - costs,
    - and orbital capabilities.
    - ENSURE COMPATIBLE WITH THE ORBIT
